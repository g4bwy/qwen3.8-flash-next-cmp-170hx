reading guide: (1) wall time per step ~= cadence column; the compute-column sums
approximate it because PP stages are serial for one stream. (2) spin/bubble is
structural for single-stream PP and shrinks as concurrency fills the pipeline; do not
optimize it directly. (3) the actionable numbers are the compute columns and the
histogram: byte-side categories (gemm/humming/marlin) vs latency/state-side
(qsa/gdn/moe-align/copy/norm/other).
